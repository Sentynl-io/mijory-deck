A Mix Shift to High-Margin Tech & SaaS

How the platform is built to scale: commerce funds the network; software compounds it.

Four wholly owned core technology pillars: BioTide and RxPath drive immediate market dominance (~$600K/mo gross path, 511+ entities, 300+ clinicians); TrakPath and Sentynl drive 85%+ gross margin expansion into enterprise-grade software and data economics. The Year 3 software crossover is where blended gross margins expand from a commerce-heavy foundation to 75%+.

Year 3 · Software crossover

At $145M illustrative total, Tech/SaaS (TrakPath + Sentynl) overtakes physical commerce (BioTide + RxPath) — 55% tech / 45% commerce. Blended gross margins move from a commerce-weighted ~35% in Year 1 to 75%+ at crossover, as TrakPath and Sentynl drive 85%+ gross margin expansion via verification SaaS and Trust Graph™ RWE data licensing.
