The Industry's Critical Vulnerability

The $1.8T wellness and longevity market is scaling rapidly, but its foundational infrastructure is fundamentally broken across six critical vectors, leading to a massive Trust Deficit.

Data & Tech

  • No software standardization: Disparate, non-communicating systems.
  • API bottlenecks: Legacy tools block tracking.
  • Security risks: Fragmented data invites breaches.

Legal & Regulatory

  • State licensing friction: Clinics face varying state laws.
  • Compliance transfer: Non-health tech risks HIPAA violations.
  • Prescription tracking: Digital relationships remain risky.

Supply Chain & QC

  • Chain of custody breaks: Complex cold-chain layers.
  • Vetting gaps: Struggle to audit 3rd-party sterile certs.
  • Physical inventory burden: Legacy operators carry manufacturing COGS, cold-chain spoilage, and inventory overhead—MiJory removes this by digitizing the trust and routing layer.

Conflicting Incentives

  • Margin compression: Intermediary costs hike prices.
  • Affiliate abuse: Pay-per-lead prioritizes payouts.
  • Volume vs. care: Fulfillment prioritized over screening.

Regulatory & Safety

  • Unapproved compounds: Lack of human clinical trials.
  • Compounding risks: Raw sales bypass drug safety limits.
  • Quality control: Dosing issues & heavy metal traces.

Consumer Risks

  • Counterfeit items: Fake online vials cause severe health risks.
  • False marketing claims: Social media trends aggressively push peptides as miracle cures without clinical proof, triggering FTC/FDA liability.
  • Lack of medical guidance: Unsupervised self-administration.
